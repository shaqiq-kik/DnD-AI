The bestiary of Dungeons and Dragons contains countless threats, each presenting unique tactical challenges. The Goblin is a common, small-sized humanoid menace. With an Armor Class of 15 when wielding a shield and a meager 7 hit points, individual goblins are fragile. They typically attack using a scimitar or shortbow. Their true danger lies in their Nimble Escape ability, which allows them to take the Disengage or Hide action as a bonus action on each of their turns. This makes them elusive skirmishers who prefer to strike from the shadows and retreat before they can be pinned down.

Skeletons are undead horrors animated by dark magic. They have an Armor Class of 13 and 13 hit points, making them relatively easy to destroy, especially since they are vulnerable to bludgeoning damage. Skeletons lack self-preservation and fight with mindless obedience, usually armed with a shortsword or a shortbow. They attack in coordinated, silent waves, following their creator's final orders until they are reduced to dust.

Orcs are fierce, aggressive raiders who thrive on combat and conquest. They possess an Armor Class of 13 and 15 hit points. Orcs use a greataxe or javelin to deal significant damage and have a defining trait called Aggressive. This trait allows an orc to use its bonus action to move up to its speed toward a hostile creature it can see. As a result, orcs close the distance with terrifying speed, overwhelming their foes with relentless, brutal melee strikes.

The Giant Spider is a large monstrosity that hunts in dark forests and deep caverns. It has an Armor Class of 14 and 26 hit points. The spider attacks with a venomous bite that deals piercing damage and forces the target to make a Constitution saving throw against potent poison. Furthermore, the Giant Spider can shoot sticky webbing to restrain its prey. It uses its Spider Climb ability to move along ceilings and walls, often dropping onto unsuspecting victims from above.

A Young Dragon, such as a young red dragon, is a massive, highly intelligent predator that commands fear and respect. With an Armor Class of 17 and over 110 hit points, it is a formidable boss monster. Dragons can attack with a devastating combination of bites and claws, but their signature weapon is their elemental breath weapon—a massive cone of destructive energy that forces all creatures caught within to make a Dexterity saving throw or suffer catastrophic damage. Young dragons are arrogant but tactical; they will use their flight to stay out of melee range while waiting for their breath weapon to recharge, swooping down only when it is advantageous.
