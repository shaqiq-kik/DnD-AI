The classes of Dungeons and Dragons fifth edition each offer unique mechanics, abilities, and playstyles tailored for various adventurers. The Fighter is a master of martial combat, proficient with all forms of armor, shields, and weapons. Relying primarily on Strength or Dexterity as their main ability score, Fighters boast a formidable d10 hit die, giving them the durability required to withstand punishment on the front lines. A key feature of the Fighter is their Action Surge, allowing them to take an additional action on their turn to swing the tide of battle, and Second Wind, providing a quick burst of self-healing.

The Wizard is the quintessential spellcaster, defined by their intense study and expansive spellbook. Wizards use Intelligence as their primary spellcasting ability and possess a meager d6 hit die, making them fragile in direct physical confrontations. However, their physical weakness is vastly overshadowed by their magical prowess. They have access to the largest spell list in the game, enabling them to unleash devastating area-of-effect spells or control the battlefield with utility magic. Wizards spend their downtime transcribing new spells and preparing their magical arsenal for the day ahead.

The Rogue is a versatile skirmisher and skill expert, favoring agility and cunning over brute force. Using Dexterity as their highest stat, Rogues rely on a d8 hit die. Their defining combat feature is Sneak Attack, which allows them to deal massive extra damage to a creature when they have advantage on the attack roll or when an ally is engaged with the target. Beyond combat, Rogues are defined by their Expertise, granting them unparalleled proficiency in skills such as Stealth, Acrobatics, and the use of thieves' tools, making them the ultimate infiltrators and trap-disarmers.

The Cleric serves as a conduit for divine magic, drawing their power directly from a chosen deity. With a d8 hit die and proficiency in medium armor and shields, Clerics are sturdy combatants who use Wisdom as their primary spellcasting ability. They are the premier healers and support characters, capable of bringing allies back from the brink of death or buffing the party's capabilities. Yet, Clerics are also formidable offensive threats, wielding radiant and necrotic magic to smite undead or blast enemies who stand against their divine cause.

Finally, the Ranger is a hybrid class that blends martial prowess with primal magic, thriving on the edges of civilization. They utilize a d10 hit die and rely on both Dexterity and Wisdom. Rangers excel at navigating the wilderness and hunting specific prey, utilizing features like Favored Enemy and Natural Explorer. In combat, they often prefer archery or two-weapon fighting, supplementing their attacks with spells like Hunter's Mark to increase their damage output. They act as the party's guides and trackers, ensuring safe passage through dangerous environments.
