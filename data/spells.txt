The magical arts in Dungeons and Dragons encompass a wide variety of effects. Fireball is one of the most iconic spells, a 3rd-level evocation that unleashes a devastating explosion. A spellcaster points their finger, and a bright streak flashes from them to a chosen point within range, blossoming into a massive explosion of flame. The explosion covers a twenty-foot radius, and all creatures within that area must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes a staggering 8d6 fire damage, or half as much on a successful one. Fireball is the ultimate tool for clearing out large groups of clustered enemies.

Magic Missile is a reliable 1st-level evocation spell that never misses. The caster creates three glowing darts of magical force that unerringly track their targets. Each dart deals 1d4 plus 1 force damage. The caster can direct the darts to hit a single target or distribute them among multiple creatures. Because Magic Missile requires no attack roll and offers no saving throw, it is an incredibly consistent way to finish off weakened enemies or break the concentration of enemy spellcasters.

Healing Word is a crucial 1st-level evocation spell for keeping allies alive in the heat of battle. Uttering a quick, mystical incantation, the caster targets a visible creature within sixty feet. The spell restores hit points equal to 1d4 plus the caster's spellcasting ability modifier. Because Healing Word is cast as a bonus action, the caster still has their main action available to attack, dodge, or cast a cantrip, making it one of the most action-efficient healing spells available during combat.

Thunderwave is a 1st-level evocation spell that acts as both an offensive weapon and a defensive tool. The caster sweeps their hands outward, creating a cube of thunderous force that originates from them. Every creature within a fifteen-foot cube must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 2d8 thunder damage and is physically pushed ten feet away from the caster. This forced movement makes Thunderwave perfect for spellcasters who find themselves surrounded and need to create distance between themselves and their attackers.

Sleep is a 1st-level enchantment spell that resolves encounters without bloodshed. The caster scatters a pinch of fine sand, sending a magical slumber over a twenty-foot radius. The spell affects creatures based on their current hit points; the caster rolls 5d8 to determine the total hit points of creatures affected. Creatures within the area are knocked unconscious in ascending order of their current hit points, starting with the weakest. It offers no saving throw, making it an exceptional spell for neutralizing groups of low-level guards or goblins quietly.
